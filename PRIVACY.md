# PovQ Studio: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 18, 2026_

PovQ Studio ("the app") is a personal content tool used only by the owner of the PovQ channel. This policy explains what data the app handles when the owner connects their social media accounts.

## Data the app accesses

When the channel owner connects an account through the platform's official login, the app may access:

- **TikTok:** basic profile info (display name, username) via `user.info.basic`; permission to publish videos via `video.publish`; view, like, and comment counts of the owner's own videos via `video.list`.
- **YouTube:** permission to upload videos and read the owner's channel name and video statistics.
- **Instagram:** permission to publish Reels and read like, comment, and view counts of the owner's own posts.

## How the data is used

- Only to publish the owner's videos and captions, and to show the owner basic statistics of those videos inside the app.
- The data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

## Storage

- Access tokens and statistics are stored locally on the owner's own computer, in files that are not published or shared.
- No data from other TikTok, YouTube, or Instagram users is collected.

## Removing access

The owner can disconnect any account at any time from inside the app, or revoke access from the platform's own settings (for TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions). Locally stored tokens are deleted when the account is disconnected.
